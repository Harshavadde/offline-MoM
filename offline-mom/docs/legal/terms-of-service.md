## AI-generated content

Summaries, minutes of meeting, key topics, and Chat answers (including the legacy Ask AI feature) are generated by a small on-device language model and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or occasionally nonsensical — see the Responsible AI and Ethical AI statements. Every Chat answer carries an on-screen "AI-generated response" notice for this reason. When Chat can't find a confident answer in your own meetings/documents, it may answer from the model's own general knowledge instead — clearly labeled as such, and never presented with a citation to your content. Action items are entered by you, the user, not generated by the AI. Do not treat any AI-generated content as authoritative without independent judgment.
