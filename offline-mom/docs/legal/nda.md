# Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) — Template

## Applicability

OfflineMoMAI's own source code, architecture, and documentation are not confidential by default (see the project README for licensing context).

Where an NDA is genuinely relevant is downstream: **teams or organizations that use OfflineMoMAI to record their own meetings** may be discussing information that *is* confidential to them (business strategy, unreleased research, personnel matters). Because OfflineMoMAI stores everything locally and shares nothing automatically, the confidentiality of that content is a matter between the organization and its own meeting participants, not something the app mediates — the template below is provided for such an organization to adapt for its own use, e.g. to have meeting participants acknowledge that a recording is being made and that its contents are confidential.

## Template

**MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into as of ______________ (date) between:

- **Disclosing Party / Organization:** ____________________
- **Recipient / Meeting Participant:** ____________________

**1. Purpose.** The parties wish to discuss matters in a meeting that will be recorded and processed using the OfflineMoMAI application, and the Recipient may receive or have access to Confidential Information as a result.

**2. Confidential Information.** Any information disclosed during the recorded meeting, or contained in the resulting transcript, summary, minutes of meeting, action items, or decisions, that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure.

**3. Obligations.** The Recipient agrees to: (a) hold Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent; (c) use it solely for the purpose stated above; (d) apply at least the same degree of care to protect it as the Recipient applies to its own confidential information of similar nature.

**4. Exclusions.** Information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient, was already known to the Recipient prior to disclosure, or is independently developed without reference to the Confidential Information, is excluded from these obligations.

**5. Term.** These obligations survive for ______ years from the date of disclosure, or indefinitely for information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law.

**6. No license.** Nothing in this Agreement grants any license or right to the Confidential Information beyond what is necessary to fulfill the stated purpose.
